## 3. A note on the zero rows in longer captures

A pane whose child has exited keeps its registry entry, and the writer keeps
recording what the sampler sees: a zombie tree (pids=1, rss=0). The series shows
the death as a cliff to zero — which is the graph working as intended, not a
sampling bug. Dead panes' rows age out through normal retention; only a *live*
pane's newest row is protected.
