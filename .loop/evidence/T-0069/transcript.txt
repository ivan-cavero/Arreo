==============================================================

retried on the 250ms->30s ramp and exhausted the relay's 3-per-10s-per-address
budget, replacing the reason with a transport error:

  registration failed: ... certificate signature does not verify under the root key
  registration failed: ... certificate signature does not verify under the root key
  registration failed: ... aborted by peer: the server refused to accept a new connection
