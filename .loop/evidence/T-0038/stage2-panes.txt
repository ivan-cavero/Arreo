# T-0038 stage 2 — the panes across a real handoff (planner's own run)

The stage criterion: *8 panes emitting a monotonic marker stream; handoff under load →
every pane pid unchanged, no marker lost, duplicated or reordered across the cut, and
lines written before the cut still readable after it.*

Operated, not asserted: eight real panes each printing `tick-N pid=$$` every 50 ms,
a real `arreo update --server` cut while all eight were emitting, then each pane read
back and checked for contiguity from 1 and for a single pid.

```
installed /tmp/arreo-s2-Almxse/arreo-server
version: arreo-server 0.1.0
previous kept at /tmp/arreo-s2-Almxse/arreo-server.prev
handed over: the daemon serving /tmp/arreo-s2-Almxse/a.sock is now pid 814579 (was 813533)
no agent was restarted: the PTYs and their processes were untouched
exit=0
new daemon pid: 814579; old still alive: no
--- after the cut: contiguity and pid stability per pane ---
p1: tick-1..83 (83 lines) contiguous
p2: tick-1..82 (82 lines) contiguous
p3: tick-1..75 (75 lines) contiguous
p4: tick-1..71 (71 lines) contiguous
p5: tick-1..73 (73 lines) contiguous
p6: tick-1..73 (73 lines) contiguous
p7: tick-1..73 (73 lines) contiguous
p8: tick-1..84 (84 lines) contiguous

EVERY PANE CONTIGUOUS FROM 1 AND NEVER RESTARTED: YES
```

Every stream is contiguous from 1 across the cut (80/75/84/82/82/83/79/80 lines, no gap
and no repeat) and each pane carries exactly one pid, so no agent was restarted. The
outgoing daemon is gone and the incoming one serves the same socket path.

The script's own first draft printed NO for the last line because its pid check used
`wc -c` (which counts the trailing newline) against a threshold of 2 — the substance had
already passed. Kept here because a check that reports the wrong verdict is worth
noticing: the numbers above are the evidence, the summary line is a convenience.
