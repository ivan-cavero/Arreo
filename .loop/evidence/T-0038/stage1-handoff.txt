## The three attacks from the security review, re-run against the fixed build

Attack 1 (F4, was CRITICAL): half-close the transfer connection.
  Before: the daemon exited 0 within 40 ms with nobody serving.
  After:  the daemon is alive and still answers a fresh client.

Attack 2 (F1, was CRITICAL): connect to .handoff without the nonce.
  Before: the attacker received the listener + lock and served the socket.
  After:  0 descriptors; mode is 0600 (measured); the daemon survives.

Attack 3 (F5, was HIGH): two handoffs at once.
  Before: both committed, two daemons served one socket (5/5 rounds).
  After:  exactly one survivor, exactly one daemon serving.
