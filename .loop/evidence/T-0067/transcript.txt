T-0067 — a self-admitted certificate that nothing can verify
==========================================================

Reproduction (from empty dirs, one box):
  1. devices list --json -> root R
  2. relay account add --root-key R -> accepted
  3. pair (print code) then pair --join (its own code) -> 'paired ... certificate: <path>'
  4. devices list --socket -> 'no devices paired yet', with TWO certs in identity/devices/
  5. daemon -> 'relay registration failed: ... certificate signature does not verify
     under the root key', retrying forever

Cross-check (the obvious explanation is ruled out):
  relay.db account.root_key   = b7982e04edd07dc116f300e2c6cf248bb824a7570f841e7cd5b219f70374681a
  devices list --json .root   = b7982e04edd07dc116f300e2c6cf248bb824a7570f841e7cd5b219f70374681a
  -> identical. The cert is not signed by the registered root.

Established: issuance path (authority.rs:268 signs with self.root; main.rs:2679 calls it),
not key resolution. What is NOT established: which of the two differs at issue time, or whether
the signed payload differs from what DeviceCert::verify recomputes. See the task file.
