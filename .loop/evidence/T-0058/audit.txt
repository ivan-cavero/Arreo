## 7. One bench reading under host contention, and why it is not a code signal

A first bench run immediately after the slices reported sampler_sweep_30panes_ms
= 307 ms against a 300 ms budget (and 730 ms for the whole 10-pane setup, vs
385-488 ms in every prior run). The host is shared: load average 7.3 with
unrelated processes (clickhouse, orca-ide, other checkouts) at 10-27% CPU.
An isolated re-run under the same load average returned 71 ms - inside the
65-83 ms band of every previous run. The metric is a wall-clock sweep over
30 panes, so it measures contention as well as code; the confirming re-run is
the number recorded above. T-0058 touches the pairing flow and the invite URI,
nothing on the sampler path.
