T-0061 — remote panes are not second-class in the sidebar
======================================================

## 1. The sidebar names the machine and the link, and shows the question

Real arreo-tui on a real pty, pointed at a peer machine over a real relay,
reached BY NAME (--machine peer-machine), frame 04 of the relay slice:

┌peer-machine · relay──────┐┌(no focus — Enter on a sidebar row)───────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│◉ question                ││                                                                                          │
│  asking-pane   3M █░░░░░░││                                                                                          │
│    Proceed with the dep… ││                                                                                          │
│● working                 ││                                                                                          │
│  remote-pane   3M █░░░░░░││                                                                                          │

The title is the session label (peer-machine · relay, resolved through the
account's directory); the question is indented under the pane it belongs to
and cut with an ellipsis because the sidebar is narrower than the sentence.
