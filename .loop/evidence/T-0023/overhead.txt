== T-0023 overhead (release) ==
QUIC connect (transport):     3.006476ms
QUIC + Noise handshake:       5.063194ms
Noise adds:                   2.056718ms (1.6840959315823576x the QUIC connect)
1 MB through the transport:   9.732935ms
RSS, listener + 5 sessions:   22680 KB

Method: a throwaway `cargo run --release` example (since deleted) that ran the
real daemon plus a real loopback QUIC listener, then took the median of 20
samples per figure. "QUIC connect" is connect + open stream with no Noise, so
"QUIC + Noise" minus it is exactly the round trip the Noise-KK handshake adds.
"1 MB" is a single 1 MB write through a live SecureChannel (the pump seals it in
60 KB messages). RSS is /proc/self/status VmRSS with the listener plus five live
sessions in the same process.
