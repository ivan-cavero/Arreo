# T-0054 evidence — a device's departure is announced to its peers

The headline measurement: a reconnect after a killed client, before and after.

| | T-0032's turn (no notice) | After T-0054 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| reconnect to a peer that had not noticed the drop | failed every attempt for **>60 s** (the test was re-scoped rather than claimed) | **77 ms** |

The fix was two-part, and the second part was the deeper bug: the relay now announces
departures (`RelayKind::PeerGone`), and a dropped `RelaySession` now closes its connection
(its pump tasks are aborted on drop). Without the second, the relay never learned the device
had left, so the notice had nothing to announce.
