## 6. The deny allow-list correction (same turn)
Declaring the relay AGPL made cargo-deny fail its license gate — the allow-list had no AGPL entry, because before this task no crate in the tree declared one. The fix keeps the dependency ban untouched: AGPL-3.0-or-later is allowed as a *first-party* license (the relay crate itself), and the workspace_deps gate asserts the per-crate side (every shipped crate Apache, the relay AGPL) which cargo-deny cannot express. cargo-deny cannot say allowed-for-one-crate, so the two gates divide the work and the evidence for both is here.
