# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct (v2.1, abridged)

Arreo is a tool about making many agents and many humans collaborate without stepping on each
other. This project applies the same standard to its own community.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Arreo community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

**Positive behavior includes:** using welcoming and inclusive language; being respectful of
differing viewpoints; accepting constructive criticism; focusing on what is best for the
community; showing empathy toward other community members.

**Unacceptable behavior:** sexualized language or imagery; trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, personal or political attacks; public or private harassment; publishing others'
private information (doxxing); conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional
setting.

## Scope

Applies in all project spaces — code, issues, PRs, Discord, events — and when representing
the project publicly. Applies equally to human and machine-authored interactions: an agent
loop's output is its operator's behavior; abuse via automated contributions is treated as
abuse by the person running the loop.

## Enforcement

Report to **<conduct@arreo.dev>** (confidential, handled by the maintainers; conflicts of
interest are recused). Consequences follow the
[full Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/):
clarification → warning → temporary ban → permanent ban for severe or repeated violations.
